# Privacy policy: Train Me on AI for GCC by ATeam

Effective date: 30 October 2026

This policy covers the "Train Me on AI for GCC by ATeam" plugin (`ateam-train-me-on-ai-gcc`), published by ATeam Soft Solutions.

## What the plugin is

The plugin contains only written instructions for AI tools (skills) and a reference library. It's built for Claude and OpenAI Codex, and may load in other tools that support the same format. It has no servers, hooks, scripts or other code that runs on its own. ATeam doesn't run any server or service for it.

## Data ATeam collects

None. No analytics, telemetry or accounts. ATeam receives nothing from your use of the plugin.

## Data the skills handle in your AI tool

When you use the skills, you share information about your business with your AI tool: your tasks, examples of your work, documents, spreadsheets and proposals. The skills:

- tell the AI to keep passwords, ID numbers, bank and card details, health information and people's personal details out of the conversation, and to help you remove names and IDs before you share anything
- keep your progress in an "AI plan" document: in your own folder if your tool can write files, otherwise as a document you save yourself
- tell the AI never to put sensitive data into the AI plan

What you share is processed under the terms of the AI tool and model provider you use (for example Anthropic or OpenAI) and your account settings, including whether your conversations may be used to train models. ATeam has no access to it.

## Outside services

The plugin doesn't send data to any outside service. If you ask for research, or the AI checks current prices, your AI tool may search the public web. The skills tell the AI never to put your customers', staff's or partners' names, your figures or confidential details into searches.

## Retention

ATeam keeps nothing. Your AI plan stays wherever you save it until you delete it.

## Children

Not intended for anyone under 18.
