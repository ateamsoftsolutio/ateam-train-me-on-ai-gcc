# Use-case catalogue

Uses of AI for small and mid-sized businesses, by function, then by industry. Pick only the ones that match the person's own tasks and problems.

**Routes:** **A** = do it yourself today in an AI chat tool. **B** = needs a no-code tool, an add-on or a connector. **C** = needs a build partner.
**Risk:** **Low** = internal drafts a person checks. **Medium** = customer-facing text or business decisions, with a person checking. **High** = personal, health or payment data, automated customer messages, money movements or regulated work.

There are no "typical savings" figures here on purpose: value always comes from the person's own minutes, volumes and costs.

**Connected tools and personal data:** a Route B tool that holds customers' contact details (a connected inbox, a follow-up tool, automated reminders) is Medium risk only on a business account, with a data processing agreement and a person approving every message. Otherwise score it High.

## Common GCC business problems

Start here when a GCC owner brings a problem. Facts about rules, deadlines and official portals come only from `gcc-business-guide.md` and `privacy-laws.md` (both in the `train-me-on-ai` skill's references), never from memory.

| Problem | Uses that help (route) | Who must check | Risk |
|---|---|---|---|
| "We can't keep up with WhatsApp enquiries" | Reply drafts in Arabic and English (A); a library of approved answers (A); a shared inbox that drafts replies for a person to approve (B); a WhatsApp assistant that hands over to a person (C) | A person approves every reply; customers' contact details stay out of personal accounts | Medium (A, and B on a business account with a data processing agreement); High (C) |
| "Our customers and documents are in Arabic and English" | Bilingual drafts and translations with checks (A); bilingual templates (A); summaries of Arabic or English contracts (A) | A native speaker for customer-facing text; a lawyer for what a contract means | Medium |
| "Tenders take us days" | Reading a tender from an official portal, a compliance list of every requirement, and a response outline (A); a library of past answers (B) | The bid team checks every requirement against the tender itself | Medium |
| "VAT and e-invoicing paperwork" | First, what the rules require and by when, from the guide (not an option: see "Required, with its date" in `SKILL.md`). Then: explaining the official rules in plain words, from the guide and its official links (A); readiness checklists and questions for the accountant and the e-invoicing provider (A); checking sample invoices against the customer's purchase order and the business's own checklist, with names and bank details removed and registration numbers swapped for made-up ones (A); a check of purchase-order numbers and prices before invoices go out (B, on a business account with a data processing agreement); connecting the accounting system to the e-invoicing provider, by the provider (C). What an e-invoice must contain comes from the provider and the official texts, never from memory | Their accountant or tax adviser, every time | Medium (A and B); High (C, the regulated act itself) |
| "Workforce-nationalisation reports and HR paperwork" | A calendar and checklists of reporting tasks from the official programme pages (A); job advertisements for nationals (A); a dashboard from the HR system (B or C) | Their HR lead or PRO; staff data stays out of chat tools | Medium (A); High (B or C, staff data) |
| "Our staff speak many languages" | Instructions, notices and announcements in staff languages such as Hindi, Urdu, Malayalam or Tagalog (A) | A native speaker for anything about safety, pay or contracts | Low to Medium |
| "Ramadan, Eid and national days" | A seasonal content calendar and campaigns (A); rotas and opening hours for Ramadan (A), using the official hours in the guide | A person checks cultural fit; HR checks hours | Low to Medium |
| "Letters and renewals with authorities" | Drafts of letters and emails to authorities, and checklists of documents for renewals (A) | Their PRO or the authority's own website | Medium |
| "Where should our data be kept?" | Research on in-region hosting options before buying (A), from the guide and `privacy-laws.md` | An adviser for personal or health data | Low (research); the decision itself is for an adviser |
| "AI isn't good enough in Arabic for us" | Testing tools on their own Arabic samples, with names removed (A); comparing tools, including Arabic models listed in the guide (A or B) | A native speaker judges the results | Low |

## By function

### Sales

| Use | What it does | Route | Risk | How to measure |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Enquiry replies | Drafts replies to email or WhatsApp enquiries from the business's own facts and tone | A (drafts); B (connected inbox, a person approves) | Medium | Minutes per reply; time to first response |
| Quotation drafts | Turns a request and a price list into a quotation and cover note from a template | A | Medium | Minutes per quotation; quotations sent a week |
| Follow-ups | Reminds and drafts follow-ups for open quotations and leads | B | Medium | Follow-ups done on time; quotations won |
| Meeting notes into updates | Turns call or meeting notes into next steps and CRM updates | A (notes); B (CRM update) | Medium | Minutes per update |
| Tender reading | Summarises a tender's requirements, deadlines and scoring, and drafts the response outline | A | Medium | Hours per tender |
| Price and competitor research | Researches market prices and competitors with sources | A (needs web search) | Low | Hours per research task |

| Retail and e-commerce | Product descriptions at scale (B); review analysis; promotions; reorder suggestions (C) | Customer data stays out of personal accounts |

| Car dealers and parts | Enquiry replies; parts quotations from catalogues (B or C); test-drive and service booking assistants (C); service reminders (B) | Customer-facing assistants need a person to take over easily |

| Real estate | Listing descriptions; enquiry handling (B or C); summaries of tenancy contracts (A, checked) | Contract meaning goes to a lawyer |
| Hospitality and food | Menu descriptions; review replies; rotas; supplier price checks | Allergen information is always checked by a person |
| Professional services | Proposals; research; client updates; questions answered from past work (B or C) | Client confidentiality comes first |
| Construction and interiors | Site report summaries; quotation and quantity summaries; client updates; quantities from drawings (C) | Quantities and prices are always checked against the drawings |
| Education and training centres | Course material; enquiry replies; timetables | Students' personal data stays out of personal accounts |
| Manufacturing | Procedures and checklists; quality report summaries; maintenance log analysis (B or C) | Safety-critical steps are always checked by a qualified person |
