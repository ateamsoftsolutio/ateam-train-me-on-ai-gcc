## 4. Estimating usage costs

Use this only to sanity-check running costs. Show the person the result and your assumptions in plain words; keep the token arithmetic for build briefs and for vendors.

**Tokens.** In English, one token is about four characters, or three-quarters of a word: 100 tokens ≈ 75 words. Other languages often need more tokens per word ([OpenAI Help, "What are tokens and how to count them?"](https://help.openai.com/en/articles/4936856-what-are-tokens-and-how-to-count-them)). Token counts also differ between models: Anthropic notes its newer models produce about 30% more tokens for the same text ([Claude pricing](https://platform.claude.com/docs/en/about-claude/pricing)).

**Method.** Monthly usage cost = conversations (or tasks) a month × (input tokens × input price + output tokens × output price) ÷ 1,000,000, where the tokens are the totals across all the AI's replies in one conversation.

- **Add up every reply the AI writes in a conversation.** Each reply re-reads its instructions, any reference material (price lists, policies) and the conversation so far, so later replies read more. Count each token once, in these per-conversation totals.
- **Output** is what the model writes back, including any hidden thinking the provider bills.
- **Assistants that take several steps** call the model several times per reply: multiply by the number of steps.
- **Use a range,** across the price bands below, unless the proposal names the model.
- **Real costs can be higher** than any estimate. The only reliable figure is measured usage from a pilot, so ask for it.
- **If no one can give sizes,** use one of these assumptions and say so: a simple enquiry or FAQ conversation, the worked example's sizes below (12,000 tokens read and 800 written in total); a booking or multi-step assistant, which reads longer instructions and results from other systems (such as free appointment slots), about 25,000 read and 1,500 written, already allowing for a few steps.
- **Arabic:** token counts per word are often higher than in English and vary by model. If conversations are mostly in Arabic, say the estimate may be low, and ask the vendor for measured token counts.

**Price bands** (from section 3): smaller models $0.10 to $0.30 input and $0.50 to $2.50 output; mid-range models $0.75 to $5 input and $3.75 to $30 output; the largest models $10 input and $50 output.

**Worked example** (illustrative assumptions; the 26 working days and the token sizes are this example's, so use the person's own figures, and if they haven't said how many days they work, ask, or show the cost per 1,000 conversations instead): 150 WhatsApp enquiries a day × 26 working days = 3,900 conversations a month. Assume each conversation has four AI replies that together read 12,000 tokens (instructions and a price list of about 2,000 tokens each time, plus the growing conversation) and write 800.

| Price band | Sum per month | Monthly usage cost |
|---|---|---|
| Smaller models | 3,900 × (12,000 × $0.10 to $0.30 + 800 × $0.50 to $2.50) ÷ 1,000,000 | about $6 to $22 |
| Mid-range models | 3,900 × (12,000 × $0.75 to $5 + 800 × $3.75 to $30) ÷ 1,000,000 | about $47 to $328 |
| Largest models | 3,900 × (12,000 × $10 + 800 × $50) ÷ 1,000,000 | about $624 |

What this shows: at these assumptions, AI usage can be a modest part of a running cost. Servers, the messaging channel, monitoring, support and the vendor's margin may cost more, which is why `ai-proposal-check` asks for a breakdown. Channel charges (for example WhatsApp Business messaging fees, SMS or phone minutes) are separate from AI usage and aren't in this library: ask the vendor or the channel provider for them.

## 6. Choosing an account (neutral guidance)

- **Start with the tool they already have.** Most sessions work on any of them.
- **For amber data,** use a business or team account (section 2), or a personal account with training switched off.
- **Compare by the features their tasks need:** file upload and analysis, web search, saved instructions or projects, connections to email and files, quality in their languages, and admin controls for a team.
- **Cost per month = seats × price per seat.** For a team, start with the people who'll use it most and measure the time saved before adding seats.
- **Check the commitment.** Choose monthly billing where it's offered. Some "monthly" prices still need a year's commitment (Microsoft 365 Copilot), and team accounts have seat minimums (two for Claude Team and ChatGPT Business, twenty for Claude Enterprise).
- Don't recommend a vendor for any reason other than fit. If two tools fit, say so.
