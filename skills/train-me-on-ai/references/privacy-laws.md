# Privacy-law notes for GCC business owners

**Checked on 30 October 2026.** Short notes for a business owner deciding whether customers', patients' or staff's personal data may go into an AI tool, which usually means it's processed outside their country. This is not legal advice: for real decisions about personal data, especially health data, they should ask a data protection adviser or lawyer. Sources marked "law firm" are law firms' summaries, with their dates, not the official texts.

## The safe default everywhere

Keep personal data out of AI chat tools by default, and health data always. Connecting a work mailbox or shared files to a business account follows "Connecting tools" in `data-safety.md`, after checking the notes for their country below. Anything more, such as a customer chat assistant or a clinical system, belongs in a system built and approved for it, with the right contracts and any permits (see "What to tell the person"). Remove names and IDs, or use made-up examples (`data-safety.md`).

## By country

### United Arab Emirates

- **Law:** Federal Decree-Law No. 45 of 2021 on the Protection of Personal Data, in force since 2 January 2022 ([official text](https://uaelegislation.gov.ae/en/legislations/1972/download)). As of March 2026 its Executive Regulations hadn't been issued and the UAE Data Office wasn't yet fully operational (law firm: [Chambers, 2026 guide](https://practiceguides.chambers.com/practice-guides/data-protection-privacy-2026/uae)).
- **Sensitive data** includes health, genetic and biometric data, religious beliefs and criminal records ([official text](https://uaelegislation.gov.ae/en/legislations/1972/download)).
- **Sending data abroad:** allowed to countries the UAE Data Office approves as giving adequate protection, or on listed grounds such as the person's explicit consent, where this doesn't conflict with the State's public or security interests ([official text](https://uaelegislation.gov.ae/en/legislations/1972/download)). Don't decide for yourself that a country is "safe".
- **Health data** falls under Federal Law No. 2 of 2019, under which health data may not be stored, processed, generated or transferred outside the UAE except as the health authorities permit (law firm: [Chambers, 2026 guide](https://practiceguides.chambers.com/practice-guides/data-protection-privacy-2026/uae)). In practice: no patient data in AI tools processed abroad.
- **Free zones** have their own laws: the DIFC Data Protection Law (DIFC Law No. 5 of 2020) and the ADGM Data Protection Regulations 2021 (law firm: [Chambers, 2026 guide](https://practiceguides.chambers.com/practice-guides/data-protection-privacy-2026/uae)).

### Saudi Arabia

- **Law:** the Personal Data Protection Law (Royal Decree M/19 of 1443H, as amended), in force since 14 September 2023; the one-year transition period ended on 14 September 2024. The regulator is the Saudi Data and Artificial Intelligence Authority, SDAIA (law firms: [DLA Piper, Feb 2026](https://www.dlapiperdataprotection.com/index.html?c=SA&t=law); [Morgan Lewis, Sep 2024](https://www.morganlewis.com/pubs/2024/09/saudi-arabia-personal-data-protection-law-transition-period-ends-september-14)).
- **Sensitive data** includes health, genetic, biometric, credit and location data (law firm: [DLA Piper, Feb 2026](https://www.dlapiperdataprotection.com/index.html?c=SA&t=law)).
- **Sending data abroad:** under a separate transfer regulation, allowed to countries with adequate protection or with safeguards such as standard contractual clauses, and transfer impact assessments are expected (law firm: [Morgan Lewis, Sep 2024](https://www.morganlewis.com/pubs/2024/09/saudi-arabia-personal-data-protection-law-transition-period-ends-september-14)). Transfers must be limited to the minimum data needed (law firm: [DLA Piper, Feb 2026](https://www.dlapiperdataprotection.com/index.html?c=SA&t=law)).
- **Watch:** SDAIA is consulting from 6 October to 5 November 2026 on draft amendments to the PDPL Implementing Regulations, including an express duty to store personal data in the Kingdom. If approved, they take effect 60 days after publication. They are proposals, not law (law firm: [Clyde & Co, Oct 2026](https://www.clydeco.com/en/insights/2026/10/sdaia-publishes-draft-amendments-to-the-pdpl)).

### Oman

- **Law:** the Personal Data Protection Law, Royal Decree 6/2022, in force since 13 February 2023, with its Executive Regulation (Ministerial Decision 34/2024); the grace period ended on 5 February 2026. The regulator is the Ministry of Transport, Communications and Information Technology, MTCIT (law firms: [Al Tamimi, Feb 2023](https://www.tamimi.com/news/new-omani-personal-data-protection-law-comes-into-force/); [CMS, Feb 2026](https://cms.law/en/omn/legal-updates/oman-personal-data-protection-law-entering-the-enforcement-phase)).
- **Amended by Royal Decree 68/2026,** in force since 7 September 2026. The law now covers personal data of people in Oman wherever it's processed, and the definition of health data is wider. Consent, where it's needed, must be explicit, and four new grounds allow processing without it. People can object to decisions made by automated processing and ask for a human review (law firm: [Al Tamimi, Oct 2026](https://www.tamimi.com/law-update/the-technology-issue-2026/articles/from-local-compliance-to-global-reach-omans-bold-new-data-protection-law/)).
- **Sensitive data,** including health data, may be processed only with a permit from MTCIT (law firm: [Al Tamimi, Feb 2023](https://www.tamimi.com/news/new-omani-personal-data-protection-law-comes-into-force/)). The 2026 amendment adds some exceptions for employees' data (law firm: [Al Tamimi, Oct 2026](https://www.tamimi.com/law-update/the-technology-issue-2026/articles/from-local-compliance-to-global-reach-omans-bold-new-data-protection-law/)).
- **Sending data abroad:** needs explicit consent, and the recipient's protection must be at least equal to Oman's, except under an international agreement Oman has joined, or where the person's identity is concealed (law firm: [DLA Piper, Mar 2024](https://www.dlapiper.com/en/insights/publications/2024/03/omans-leap-towards-data-protection-unveiling-the-new-implementing-regulation)). We haven't confirmed whether the 2026 amendment changed these rules.
- **Sensitive data abroad:** MTCIT's FAQ and the Cyber Defence Centre system ([Royal Decree 64/2020, Art. 4(5)](https://qanoon.om/p/2020/rd2020064/); [MTCIT FAQ](https://mtcit.gov.om/sectors/governance/personal), both checked on 17 October 2026) say the Cyber Defence Centre's approval is needed before sensitive data, such as health data, is stored or processed outside Oman. A law firm describes the approval as one that "may be required" (law firm: [CMS, Feb 2026](https://cms.law/en/omn/legal-updates/oman-personal-data-protection-law-entering-the-enforcement-phase)).

### Qatar

- **Law:** Law No. 13 of 2016 Concerning Personal Data Protection (known as the PDPPL), which took effect in 2017 (law firm: [Chambers, 2026 guide](https://practiceguides.chambers.com/practice-guides/data-protection-privacy-2026/qatar)).
- **Sensitive data,** including physical or mental health, may not be processed without the regulator's authorisation (law firms: [DLA Piper, Jan 2024](https://www.dlapiperdataprotection.com/index.html?c=QA&t=law); [Chambers, 2026 guide](https://practiceguides.chambers.com/practice-guides/data-protection-privacy-2026/qatar)).
- **Sending data abroad** is generally not to be restricted unless the processing breaks the law or could cause serious harm to the person (law firm: [DLA Piper, Jan 2024](https://www.dlapiperdataprotection.com/index.html?c=QA&t=law)).
- The **Qatar Financial Centre** has its own Data Protection Regulations 2021 (law firm: [Chambers, 2026 guide](https://practiceguides.chambers.com/practice-guides/data-protection-privacy-2026/qatar)).

### Bahrain

- **Law:** Law No. 30 of 2018 with respect to Personal Data Protection, in force since 1 August 2019 (law firm: [DLA Piper, Feb 2026](https://www.dlapiperdataprotection.com/index.html?c=BH&t=law)).
- **Sensitive data** includes health data. Processing it needs consent, with some exceptions including healthcare (law firm: [DLA Piper, Feb 2026](https://www.dlapiperdataprotection.com/index.html?c=BH&t=law)).
- **Sending data abroad** is allowed only to countries on the authority's list, with the authority's permission, or under exceptions such as consent (law firm: [DLA Piper, Feb 2026](https://www.dlapiperdataprotection.com/index.html?c=BH&t=law)).

### Kuwait

- **No single general data-protection law** as of March 2026 (law firm: [Chambers, 2026 guide](https://practiceguides.chambers.com/practice-guides/data-protection-privacy-2026/kuwait)).
- The **CITRA Data Privacy Protection Regulation** (Resolution No. 26 of 2024) applies to service providers licensed by CITRA (law firm: [Al Tamimi, Feb 2024](https://tamimi.com/news/communications-and-information-technology-regulatory-authority-has-issued-new-data-privacy-regulations)). Sources differ on its reach: one says it also covers anyone running a website, app or cloud service (law firm: [DLA Piper, Feb 2025](https://www.dlapiperdataprotection.com/index.html?c=KW&t=law)); another, only licensed telecom and internet providers (law firm: [Chambers, 2026 guide](https://practiceguides.chambers.com/practice-guides/data-protection-privacy-2026/kuwait)).
- **Sending data abroad:** CITRA licensees must tell users if their data will go abroad, naming the countries, and may need CITRA's approval. Other businesses fall under the E-Transactions Law's duties on consent, purpose and security (law firms: [Chambers, 2026 guide](https://practiceguides.chambers.com/practice-guides/data-protection-privacy-2026/kuwait); [DLA Piper, Feb 2025](https://www.dlapiperdataprotection.com/index.html?c=KW&t=law)).
- **Medical information** generally needs the patient's written consent before disclosure, under Medical Profession Law No. 70 of 2020 (law firm: [Chambers, 2026 guide](https://practiceguides.chambers.com/practice-guides/data-protection-privacy-2026/kuwait)).

## What to tell the person

1. Keep personal data out of AI chat tools by default, and health data always.
2. If the business genuinely needs AI on personal data (for example a customer chat assistant or a clinical system), that's a project for a build partner and an adviser: the right account or contract, where the data is processed, consent wording, and any permits or approvals.
3. These notes are dated. Laws in this region are changing quickly, so check before relying on them.

## Dates to watch

- **Saudi Arabia:** SDAIA's consultation on draft amendments closes on 5 November 2026; any approved changes take effect 60 days after publication.
- **UAE:** the PDPL's Executive Regulations, not issued as of March 2026; organisations get six months to comply once they are.
