# Changelog

## 1.0.0 (October 2026)

- First release, as Train Me on AI for GCC. Built for Claude (claude.ai, the desktop app and Claude Code) and OpenAI Codex.
- Three skills: `train-me-on-ai`, `ai-opportunity-map` (with a one-problem mode) and `ai-proposal-check`.
- Reference library dated 30 October 2026: six core sessions and seven optional modules, a use-case catalogue with common GCC business problems, a GCC business guide (tax and e-invoicing, workforce nationalisation, government AI guidance, Arabic AI models and in-region hosting, tender portals, working week and Ramadan hours), a proposal checklist, data-safety rules, AI prices and privacy-law notes for the six GCC countries and their financial free zones.
